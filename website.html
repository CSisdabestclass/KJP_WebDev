Basketball De-Brief

Basketball is one of the most famous and watched sports in the world. It was invented in 1891 by a man named Dr. James Naismith. Its a game where you try to get a ball into the net on the other side of the court while the opposing team is trying to stop you and your team. Every time you get the basketball into the hoop (net) you get 2 points. If you are far enough, you get 3 points instead of 2. There are 5 people per team and the game is split into 2 halfs; both are 24 minutes long. Who ever has the most points after the 2nd half, their team wins.

Main Rules

Basketball has a lot of rules but you just need to know the main one. The main rule is that you can't run while holding the ball. If you do, its called a "travel". The way you can move around with the ball is by dribbling. This is when you bounce the ball on the ground as you run/walk. Once you stop dribbling, you have to stop walking as well. If you start dribbling after you held it, it would be considered a "travel". Also, if you jump while holding the ball, thats a "travel" but if you get rid of the ball before you hit the ground (like shooting or passing), than your OK.
